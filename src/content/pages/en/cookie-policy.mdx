Cookies are small text files that a site stores in the browser of its visitors. This page explains which cookies and similar tools herohubs.it uses and how you can manage them. The data controller is Ranieri Vannozzi, as stated in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy).

### Technical storage

The site stores a single technical value in the browser's local storage, called `cookiesAccepted`, which remembers whether you accepted or rejected statistics so the banner is not shown again on every page. It contains no personal data, stays on your device until you delete it and does not require consent, because it exists to respect your choice.

### Statistics cookies

Only after your consent does the site enable Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, which sets these cookies:

- `_ga`, which distinguishes visitors pseudonymously, lasting 2 years
- `_ga_<ID>`, which keeps the session state, lasting 2 years

If you reject, Google Analytics is not loaded and none of these cookies is set. The data collected is used only for aggregate visit statistics, not for profiling or advertising. More details are in [Google's notice](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### What the site does not use

The site does not use profiling cookies, advertising cookies or social network pixels.
